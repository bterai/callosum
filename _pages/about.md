Founded by a team with deep expertise in sensing, estimation, and control of dynamical systems, Callosum AI was created to bring creative intelligence to consulting and product development. Our work ranges from optimizing human body dynamics for injury prevention and performance enhancement to developing high-precision control systems for robotics and flight dynamics.

## Mission & Vision

At Callosum AI, our mission is to harness advanced modelling techniques to solve real-world challenges. Our vision of "Creative Intelligence" inspires us to innovate in areas such as biomechanics, robotics, and safety-critical applications.
