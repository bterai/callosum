Callosum AI brings extensive expertise across several advanced fields. Our work is divided into key areas:

## Biomechanics & Human Body Dynamics

- **Focus:** Injury prevention, performance benchmarking, and energy expenditure analysis.  
- **Notable Works:**  
  - Postural stability characterization in shipboard and high-shock marine environments.  
  - Power estimation for cycling biomechanics.

## Robotics & Motion Control

- **Focus:** Kinematic and dynamic modelling for high-precision, real-time applications.  
- **Applications:**  
  - Camera robotics optimized for noise and vibration prone settings.  
  - SATCOM COTM (Communication on the move) antenna systems and aircraft actuator controls.

## Flight Dynamics & Simulation

- **Focus:** Simulation and modelling for flight dynamics and control.  
- **Applications:**  
  - Inertial navigation systems, air data systems, and flight control systems.

## Mining & Heavy Equipment Dynamics

- **Focus:** Real-time sensing for enhanced operator safety and productivity.  
- **Notable Works:**  
  - Kinematic and dynamic modelling of mining shovels and excavators;
  - Real-time payload monitoring and dig force characterization to optimize mining operations.
